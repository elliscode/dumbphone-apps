How it works

Schedule an SMS reminder. If you remember on your own, press SKIP and the next text won't be sent. DEFER also pushes it back, but doesn't count against the number of reminders, so a daily reminder limited to 1 works as a "send this if I stop checking in" switch. Times are Eastern (America/New_York).
